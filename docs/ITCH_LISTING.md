## Description (paste into the page)

Ask anything. Socrates does not hand down answers: he asks, and you think. Diogenes, Plato and Aristotle each have their own way of talking with you.

The philosophers are artificial characters, imagined alive in today's world and made from what was written about them and after them. They are not the real people, and they say so. They are not a medical, legal or crisis service.

**How it works**
- Choose a philosopher and write, in English or Greek.
- He answers briefly, often with a question, and holds you to your own words.
- Stuck? "Help me answer" offers a few ways to begin, and you may change them.
- Now and then a philosopher puts a question to you that he thinks is worth thinking all the way through. Think enough of them through with one philosopher, and you meet the next (in this beta all four are open).
- Each philosopher keeps short notes about what you said, only for himself. You can read and delete every one, or switch the memory off.

**Private by design**
- Your conversations stay on your computer, in an encrypted store. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, and no Socrates server.
- Your messages go from your computer to the AI provider you chose, with your own key. A small card with a message from the project or a sponsor may appear at the foot of the screens, never inside a conversation.
- Several people can share one computer: each has a profile with its own folder, key and optional passphrase. A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered. If you tick "Keep me signed in on this computer", the passphrase is kept in your system's secure storage until you log out.
- Free and open source (MIT). Source: https://github.com/Nireus79/Socrates-ai

**You need your own key**
Socrates works with a Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI API key that you paste in at setup; the app explains how to get one, with buttons to the providers' pages. A chat subscription does not include an API key. Your messages are sent to the provider under its terms, so set a spending limit there. On a computer you can instead connect a model running on your own machine, but this is experimental: the philosophers speak from what the model already knows of their books, and a small model often misremembers them, invents details and writes poor Greek, and the safety checks are less reliable. Claude or OpenAI is recommended.

**Install**
- **Windows** (Intel or AMD 64-bit PC): unzip it into a folder and run `socrates-desktop.exe`; it offers to add itself to your Start menu. The beta is not signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": choose "More info", then "Run anyway". It needs the WebView2 runtime (included in Windows 11). The author has run the Windows build on a Windows computer. There is no build for ARM Windows yet; it may run through Windows' own emulation, which has not been tried.
- **Linux** (Intel or AMD 64-bit): unpack the archive and run `socrates-desktop`; it offers to add itself to your applications menu, with its icon. It needs GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1 (`libgtk-3-0`, `libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0`). There is no build for ARM Linux yet.
- **macOS** (Intel and Apple Silicon): unzip it and drag Socrates.app to Applications. The beta is not notarised by Apple, so the first start is refused: open System Settings, Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway". **The author has not run the macOS build on a Mac yet**, so it may have problems; please report them.
- **Android** (7 or newer; 64-bit ARM phones, which is almost every phone made after 2017, and x86_64 devices and emulators): download the signed APK from this page, open it, and allow installing from your browser or file manager when your phone asks. It is the same file as on GitHub (https://github.com/Nireus79/Socrates-ai/releases), so it updates over it. Closing the app stops its engine. It may also come to F-Droid once it is listed there.
- Remove: close it and delete the folder; your data stays in the `.socrates` folder of your user folder (export first in Settings if you want to keep it, then delete that folder). On Android, uninstalling the app removes everything.
