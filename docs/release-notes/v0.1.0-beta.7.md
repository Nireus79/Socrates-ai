Free beta of Socrates, an app for talking with four artificial Greek philosophers: Socrates, Diogenes, Plato and Aristotle. You need your own Claude or OpenAI key. Everything is stored encrypted on your device; there is no Socrates server, no account and no analytics.

## What changed since beta.6
- Android: fixed "The engine does not answer" and a crash on x86_64 devices and emulators found by F-Droid's tester. The app now carries engines for both arm64 and x86_64 (the file is larger).
- Computers: "Keep me signed in on this computer" on the login screen. A profile with a passphrase keeps it in the system's secure storage (Linux Secret Service, Windows Credential Manager, macOS keychain), only if you tick the box, and Log out forgets it.
- Computers: Socrates offers to add itself to your applications menu (and the desktop), with its icon, so you can start it like any program. It is also in Settings. Linux and Windows; on macOS drag Socrates.app to Applications.
- Computers: drop-down lists (language, profile, provider, model) now follow the light and dark theme instead of showing white.
- Computers: "A model on this computer" (Ollama): a reply that takes more than five minutes no longer ends with "The engine does not answer."

- "A model on this computer" is now labelled experimental, with a plain warning: the philosophers speak from what the model already knows of their books, and in a test a small local model (Mistral 7B) misremembered and invented details (for example it gave the wrong example in the first book of the Republic) and wrote nonsense in Greek. Claude or OpenAI is recommended.

## Downloads
- `socrates-v0.1.0-beta.7-android.apk` signed Android app (Android 7 or newer, arm64 and x86_64). Closing the app stops its engine.
- Linux, Windows and macOS builds, unsigned or ad-hoc signed. Check a download with the `.sha256` file next to it.

## Know before you install
- Windows: the first start shows a SmartScreen warning because the build is not signed ("More info", then "Run anyway"). It needs the WebView2 runtime (included in Windows 11). The author has run the Windows build once, on 4 November.
- macOS: the app is not notarised, so macOS will warn on the first start (right-click the app, then Open). **It has not been run on a Mac by the author yet.**
- Linux: needs GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1 (`libgtk-3-0`, `libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0`).
- The philosophers are artificial characters made from what was written about them. They are not real people and not a source of medical, legal or other professional advice. The app is for people aged 16 and over. If you are thinking of hurting yourself, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line.
- How well they talk, and how reliably the safety checks work, depend on the model behind them. You pay your AI provider for what you use; set a spending limit.
