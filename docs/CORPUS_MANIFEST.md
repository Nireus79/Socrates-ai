# Corpus manifest (2026-10-23) — SHELVED

**Shelved late on 2026-10-23 (PLAN 18): the application ships no texts. This file records what was built and the rights position of each text, in case a corpus returns.**

Status: working record of the texts built for the corpus (PLAN 13). Nothing is bundled in the repository yet; texts, index and scripts were built in a scratchpad. The rule: a translator who died before 1955, or a translation old enough that its translator cannot have lived to then,
so that the work is free in the European Union as well as in the United States; checked per work. Sources are Project Gutenberg and the Internet Archive (page scans, OCR text cleaned lightly: its errors lower retrieval quality). Rights and dates are from the catalogue records and from this
assistant's memory and are to be re-checked before release.

Total: 63 works, about 29,800 passages of about 140 words, about 30 million characters, a database of 53 MB with the full-text index (FTS5).

## Gaps that remain
- Dio Chrysostom's Diogenes discourses (the Loeb translation is probably still protected; no older English found; only the Hunters of Euboea is in).
- Plato's letters (no free translation found). Aristotle: the translations edited by W. D. Ross (1908-1931) are left out because Ross died in 1971; works not covered by an older translation (for example the Physics, Parva Naturalia, Eudemian Ethics) were not looked for yet.
- Aristotle's Metaphysics and several others come from OCR of the Bohn editions and are noisy; Plato's Seventh Letter and the Cynic epistles are absent.

## Greek originals (built 2026-10-23, scratchpad only)
Source: the Perseus canonical-greekLit and the First1KGreek repositories on GitHub (TEI texts with Stephanus, Bekker or chapter numbers), both licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (see the session record, section 28, for what that means). Corpus: 206 works, 16,807 passages, 82 MB:
Plato (all 36 works), Xenophon (4), Aristotle (about 20), Plutarch (142: the Lives and the Moralia), Epictetus (Discourses and Handbook). No free Greek text was found for Diogenes Laertius, Lucian or Julian.
Editions behind the texts, by the editor's date (the ancient text is public domain; the question is whether a modern editor holds a right in the edition):
- Plato: Burnet, 1900-1915. Xenophon: Marchant, 1921. Epictetus: Schenkl, 1916. Plutarch: Vernardakis (Teubner, 1888-1895) for the Moralia and Perrin (Harvard, 1914-1926) for the Lives. Aristotle: Bekker (1829, 1837), Bywater (1894), Susemihl (1884), Kenyon (1920), Armstrong (1935).
- To be looked at before release (a twentieth-century editor, risk higher): Aristotle's Metaphysics (Ross, 1924), Politics (Ross, 1957), Rhetoric (Ross, 1959), Poetics (Kassel, 1965) and Physics (Clarendon, 1960).

## Greek candidates (for a later Greek edition)
- Open Library (openbook.gr): Plato's Republic (Gryparis, died 1942) and Symposium (Kountouriotis) are marked Public Domain; Nirvanas's Apology is CC BY-SA (avoid); several others are marked only "free distribution" (permission needed).
- Gutenberg Greek texts: Aristophanes' Clouds (Demetrakopoulos, 1864-1922), the Athenian Constitution (Zervos, 1875-1944), Plutarch's Lives (Rankaves, 1809-1892), Lucian (Kondylakes, 1861-1920), the Nicomachean Ethics (Zambas, born 1866, death year not checked), On the Soul (Gratsiatos, rights not checked).
